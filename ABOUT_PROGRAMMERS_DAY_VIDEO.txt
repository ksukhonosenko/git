🎉 WHAT IS THIS?

This repository now contains a special celebratory video created for
Programmer's Day (День программиста) - an international holiday
celebrated on the 256th day of the year!

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
📅 ABOUT PROGRAMMER'S DAY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

• Date: September 13, 2026 (256th day of year in 2026)
• Significance: 256 = 2^8 (number of values representable by a byte)
• Origin: Officially recognized in Russia since 2009
• Celebrated: Russia, CIS countries, and internationally by developers

🚀 HOW TO USE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WATCH THE VIDEO:
   $ ffplay programmers_day_2026.mp4
   (or open with any video player)

REGENERATE THE VIDEO:
   $ pip3 install -r requirements.txt
   $ python3 create_programmers_day_video.py

CUSTOMIZE:
   Edit create_programmers_day_video.py to modify:
   - Video duration and resolution
   - Colors and animations
   - Messages and text
   - Effects and timing

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
🔧 TECHNICAL DETAILS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Technology Stack:
   • Python 3
   • Pillow (PIL) - Image rendering
   • NumPy - Mathematical calculations
   • FFmpeg - Video encoding

Process:
   1. Generate 300 PNG frames (one per 1/30th second)
   2. Each frame is procedurally rendered with animations
   3. Frames are compiled into MP4 with H.264 codec
   4. Temporary files automatically cleaned up

Performance:
   • Generation time: ~11 seconds total
   • Memory efficient (sequential processing)
   • High quality output (CRF 23)
